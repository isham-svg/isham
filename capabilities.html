From prototype to production, under one roof.

Every process — molding, decorating, coating and assembly — is performed in-house at our Virginia facility, under one ISO 9001:2015 quality system.

01

Rapid Prototyping

Functional silicone prototypes in a typical 2–3 week turnaround, so you can validate fit and feel before tooling.

02

Liquid Injection Molding

Closed-loop LSR molding for high precision, reduced cycle times and repeatable, contamination-free quality.

03

Insert Molding

Proprietary 2- and 3-shot methods that integrate keys, windows, lenses and rigid parts into one component.

04

Assembly

Over-mold assemblies and pressure-sensitive adhesive (PSA) installation, finished and inspected in-house.

05

Color Matching

Pantone (PMS) matching to a 1 Delta-E variation using both visual and electronic analysis.

06

Decorating

Screen printing with proprietary silicone inks cross-link bonded to the keypad during curing.

07

Coatings

Si Coat I (abrasion), Si Coat II (chemical) and Si Prime (bonding) protect parts in demanding use.

08

Laser Marking

Durable legends and graphics — a cost-effective decorating method for lower-volume runs.

09

Design for Manufacturability

DFM recommendations that optimize your part for efficient, cost-effective manufacturing.

Coatings & finishing

Si Coat I

Si Coat II

Chemical-resistant coating that withstands petroleum and harsh chemicals.

Si Prime

Bonding solution that reliably joins silicone with acrylic adhesives and laminates.
