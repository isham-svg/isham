Services

Everything the site needs to work.

Design, build, copy, search, speed. One team, one standard, no handing you off halfway through.

01

Website Design

Designed around your business, not dragged out of a template library. We start with what you actually need the site to do — book the class, fill the chair, get the quote request — and design outward from there. You see a homepage concept before you spend a cent.

02

Website Redesign

Keep what works. Fix what doesn't. Lose what never did. If you already rank for something, we protect it — redirects mapped, structure carried over, nothing thrown away by accident on launch day.

03

Brand Identity

A look that holds up on a website, a van, and a business card. Logo, colours, type and the rules for using them, delivered as files you own and can hand to anyone.

04

UI / UX

Every page pointed at one thing: the visitor getting in touch. Clear navigation, obvious next steps, forms short enough that people finish them. Tested on a phone, because that's where most people will be.

05

SEO

We handle the technical side so search engines understand exactly what you do. The right structure, the right signals, the right setup — headings, schema, sitemaps, page speed, and copy written for how people actually search.

06

Conversion Optimization

Traffic is not the goal. Enquiries are the goal. We look at where people drop off, what they hesitate over, and what they need to see before they trust you — then change the page to match.

07

Motion Design

Movement with a reason. Transitions that show a relationship, reveals that guide the eye down the page, and nothing that slows the site down or gets in the way of reading.

08

Landing Pages

One page, one offer, built to be measured. Useful for a campaign, a new service, or a location you want to rank for on its own.

09

Website Maintenance

Updates, backups, monitoring, and someone who answers when you write. Small content changes handled so the site doesn't quietly go stale.

10

Content Strategy

We write the copy. You approve it. Nobody stares at a blank page or fills in a spreadsheet at the weekend. We work out what each page needs to say, then say it plainly.
