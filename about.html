Our story

More than a supplier — an engineering partner.

SiTech is a US-based silicone rubber injection molding manufacturer specializing in high-quality, first-surface silicone keypads, custom parts and over-mold assemblies. We utilize a cutting-edge injection molding process in collaboration with Dow and Momentive silicones, using platinum-curing catalysts to optimize production efficiency.

Our advanced Liquid Injection Molding machines streamline processes that compression molders cannot match. Combined with proprietary 2- and 3-shot insert molding methods and closed-loop LSR systems, we deliver precision, repeatability and speed — with rapid prototyping typically completed in 2–3 weeks.

Where we are

All manufacturing happens at our facility at 140 Harwood Drive, Newport News, Virginia — a competitive US manufacturer serving medical, defense, aerospace, aviation, marine, industrial, telecommunications and computing customers.
